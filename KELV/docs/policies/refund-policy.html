Your right to return

You can withdraw from your order within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day you, or someone you name, receive the last product of the order.

What can be returned

For hygiene reasons, skincare can be returned only unopened, unused and with its seal intact. Opened products, and products whose seal has been broken, cannot be returned unless they are damaged or faulty.

Kits can be returned whole or product by product, under the same conditions.

How to return

Contact us with your order number and the products you want to return. We reply with the return address and the steps to follow. Send the products back within 14 days of telling us, well packed, so they arrive in the condition they left.

You pay the cost of sending the products back, unless they arrived damaged, faulty or were not the products you ordered.

Refunds

Once we receive the products and check them, we refund the price of the returned products and the standard delivery cost of the order, within 14 days, using the same payment method you paid with. You are not charged any fee for the refund.

If you chose a faster delivery than our standard one, we refund only the standard delivery cost.

Damaged or faulty products

If a product arrives damaged, leaks, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We replace it or refund it in full, including the delivery cost, at no cost to you.

Exchanges

We do not exchange products directly. Return the product for a refund and place a new order for the one you want.
