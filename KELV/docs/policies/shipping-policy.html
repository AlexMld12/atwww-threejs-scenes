Where we ship

We ship to the countries listed at checkout. If your country is not in the list, we cannot deliver there yet.

Processing time

Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days of the order confirmation email. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are prepared on the next business day.

Delivery times and costs

The delivery options, their cost and the estimated delivery time for your address are shown at checkout, before you pay. Delivery times are estimates and start once your order has shipped.

Orders above the free-shipping threshold shown in the cart ship free with our standard delivery.

Tracking

When your order ships, we send you an email with the tracking link. If it has not arrived within the estimated time, contact us with your order number and we will look into it with the carrier.

Customs and duties

For deliveries outside the United States, customs duties and import taxes may apply. They are set by the destination country and are paid by the recipient, unless the checkout shows them as included.

Damaged or lost parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, or is lost in transit, contact us within 14 days of the estimated delivery date. We send the products again or refund them, as set out in our Refund Policy.
